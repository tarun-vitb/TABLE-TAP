How it works

Four steps, from the table to the kitchen, with no app in between.

01

Owner adds the menu

Add items, prices and photos. Print the QR code for each table.

02

Customer scans

The phone camera opens the menu for that exact table. No download, no account.

03

Kitchen sees it live

Orders land on the kitchen display with their table number and any note.

04

Serve and bill

Mark the order served, then bill it — UPI or cash — from the dashboard.

Questions owners ask

Do customers need to install an app?

No. The QR opens the menu in the phone browser. There is no download and no customer account.

What if a restaurant has no food photos yet?

The menu still works — items show a clean placeholder tile with the dish initial until the owner uploads a photo from the menu page.

Is this for delivery?

No. TableTap is dine-in only in the MVP: scan, order, serve, bill. No riders and no aggregator integration.
