How it works

  1. Owner adds menu + tables and prints one QR per table.
  2. Customer scans, orders, and tracks the order.
  3. Kitchen marks Preparing → Ready. Waiter serves.
  4. Cashier bills Cash or UPI (the restaurant's own UPI ID).
